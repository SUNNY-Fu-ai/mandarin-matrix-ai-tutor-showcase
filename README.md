An interactive Mandarin-speaking web prototype that connects a speaking 3D character with Mandarin Matrix's graded learning materials. Learners select a book or lesson and practise its vocabulary and sentence patterns through spoken conversation.

## Project context and recording guide

This is a commissioned development project for **Mandarin Matrix**, whose graded Chinese learning materials and original characters provide the educational and visual foundation. Visit the [company's official website](https://mandarinmatrix.org/) for its products and brand context.

The four recordings document **three successive development stages**. They should be read as a progression from a rough technical prototype to an increasingly refined learner-facing demonstration.

| Stage and date shared | Purpose of the recording | How to interpret its quality and status |

| **Early public-facing prototype · 5 October 2026 · Demo A and Demo B** | Show the first learner-facing lesson interface in two teaching contexts. | **Early demonstration builds, still work in progress.** These are the orange-dominant interface recordings, before the later brand-colour revision. |

## Updated public-facing prototype · 14 October 2026

**Later iteration with the revised company-aligned colour scheme.** The recording supplied for the 14 October showcase is approximately **2 minutes 12 seconds** long. It shows the start page, the Mark lesson experience and the book-selection interface.

### Why the UI colours changed

After feedback from Mandarin Matrix, the interface was revised using the [company's official website](https://mandarinmatrix.org/) as the reference for the visual direction. The early recordings use a strong orange treatment; the later version moves to dark blue/teal headers, pale blue and white panels, and warm yellow accents. This connects the prototype more closely to the company's existing visual identity.

The change documents an iteration in the same commissioned project: moving beyond the rough engineering sandbox and the early public-facing demonstrations toward a more considered learner experience. Functional details, teaching behaviour and interaction quality continue to be refined. The newer appearance does **not** mean the public-facing product is finished.

## My contribution

- Proposed the voice-driven avatar architecture and organised the development workflow.
- Adapted Mark into 3D and worked on avatar animation, expression controls and lip-sync.
- Organised Mandarin Matrix teaching materials for the lesson and conversation pipeline.
- Integrated spoken AI interaction and developed the prototype interfaces.
- Refined the user experience and teaching flow in response to client and teacher feedback.

AI-assisted coding and modelling tools were used during development. My work included setting requirements, directing those tools, checking outputs and resolving integration problems. Mandarin Matrix supplied the original characters, illustrations and teaching content. The current product introduction describes the voice component as Grok technology from xAI.
